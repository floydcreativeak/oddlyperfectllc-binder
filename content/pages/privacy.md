## Who we are

This website belongs to Oddly Perfect LLC, a marketing and public relations firm on Alaska's Kenai Peninsula. If you have a question about this policy, call or email us using the details at the bottom of every page.

## What we collect

**Nothing you type.** The site has no contact forms, sign-ups or log-ins. If you call or email us, we receive what you choose to share, the way any phone call or email works, and we use it only to reply and to work with you.

**Visit statistics, only if you agree.** When you first visit, a small note asks whether we may use Google Analytics. If you choose "Yes," Google Analytics 4 records things like which pages were viewed, roughly where visitors are (city or region level), what kind of device and browser they used, and how they found the site. We use this to see which pages are useful and which need work. We do not use it for advertising, and we have turned off Google's advertising features for this site.

If you choose "No," or ignore the note, Google Analytics does not load at all.

## What your browser stores

A few small preferences are saved in your own browser (in "local storage") so the site behaves the way you left it:

- your light or dark theme choice
- your Google Analytics yes or no
- whether you've already seen the short opening animation this visit

These stay on your device. We can't see them, and you can clear them any time by clearing your browser's site data. If you accept analytics, Google Analytics also sets its own cookies (named `_ga` and `_ga_` followed by an ID) to tell visits apart.

## Changing your mind

Use the **Analytics settings** link in the footer to change your answer at any time. Choosing "No" stops Google Analytics from loading on future pages.

## Who else is involved

- **GitHub Pages** hosts this website. Like most web hosts, GitHub's servers may log technical details such as your IP address when your browser requests a page. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.
- **Google Analytics** (only with your consent) is provided by Google. See Google's privacy policy for how Google handles that data.

We don't sell, rent or trade personal information. Ever.

## Children

This site is meant for adults running businesses and organizations. It isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

## Changes

If we change how the site handles information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Some businesses need a marketing department and can't justify hiring one. Some have a marketing person who's drowning. Some just need a grown-up in the room to say "do this first."

We built Oddly Perfect for all three. Every client's needs are a little odd. Our job is to tailor the help until it fits perfectly: the right level of support, at a price you can see before you call.

We're friendly, we're fast, and we're unreasonably organized. We're also honest about what we don't do. We don't take kickbacks, we don't sell our relationships, and we never promise results nobody can promise.

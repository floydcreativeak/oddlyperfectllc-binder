## Our goal

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA across this website, in both the light and dark themes.

## What we've built in

- **Keyboard access everywhere.** Every link, button and interactive piece works with a keyboard, with a clearly visible focus outline. A "skip" link at the top jumps straight to the main content.
- **Readable colors.** Text and important graphics meet AA contrast in both themes, including text on every sticky-note color.
- **Less motion on request.** If your device is set to reduce motion, the site swaps movement for simple fades, skips the opening animation, and turns off the custom cursor, tilting cards, parallax and 3D effects. No content is hidden.
- **Drag-and-drop with alternatives.** Anything you can drag, like the notes on the reviews board, can also be moved with the keyboard, and the same content is available as a plain list for screen readers.
- **Described images.** Every photo has a text description, and decorative doodles are hidden from screen readers.
- **Clear structure.** Pages use proper headings, landmarks and labels, and the current page is announced in the menus.
- **Comfortable touch targets.** Buttons and links on phones are at least 44 pixels tall.
- **Works without the fancy stuff.** The 3D sticky-note pile on the home page is decoration. With it turned off, or on older devices, you get a lighter version and all of the same information.

## How we check

We test with automated tools (axe and Lighthouse) in light and dark themes, by keyboard alone, with reduced motion turned on, and on slow mobile connections. Automated tools don't catch everything, so we also review pages by hand.

## Something not working?

If anything on this site is hard to use, please tell us. Call or email using the details at the bottom of this page, tell us which page and what happened, and we'll fix it or get you the information another way.
